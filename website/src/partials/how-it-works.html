What this demonstrates

Selecting a service shows its port, local URL, process and tunnel state — the same information the real application shows, in the same layout.

Try it

  1. Pick a service

    Choose one of the three simulated listeners.

  2. Start or stop it

    The tunnel panel follows the selected service.

  3. Copy the URL

    An example address appears while a tunnel is running.

What it does not do

The ports, processes and URLs are invented for illustration. This page cannot reach your computer's network stack, read your processes, or contact Cloudflare.
